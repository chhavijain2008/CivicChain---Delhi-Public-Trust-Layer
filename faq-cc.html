08 / QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Transparency without the jargon.

Can a complaint be deleted?

No. CivicChain is add-only. If a complaint is challenged or reopened, the earlier “Resolved” event stays visible and a new event is appended.

What makes a record tamper-evident?

Each event receives a SHA-256 fingerprint and points to the previous event. Changing an old event breaks the chain and becomes detectable.

Does the map reveal my home address?

No. The public map displays approximate ward-level zones rather than exact locations.
